# Fall Unit Study - Parent Guide

## Welcome! 👋

This guide will help you get the most out of the Fall Unit Study with your K-2nd grade child(ren).

---

## How to Use This Unit Study

### Weekly Structure
- **Monday:** Introduction & Language Arts activity
- **Tuesday:** Math & Science exploration
- **Wednesday:** Art & creative project
- **Thursday:** Social Studies & discussion
- **Friday:** Movement/PE & review

### Time Commitment
- 15-30 minutes per day
- Flexible - move activities around as needed
- Mix and match based on your family's schedule

---

## Materials Needed (Budget-Friendly!)

**Free/Inexpensive items:**
- Paper and pencils
- Crayons, markers, colored pencils
- Leaves and natural materials
- Pumpkins or gourds (seasonal)
- Old magazines for collages
- Tape, glue, scissors
- Household items for activities

**NO expensive curriculum needed!**

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Start with what excites your child** - Don't do activities in order
2. **Use nature** - Go outside and collect leaves, observe weather
3. **Repeat favorite activities** - Learning happens through repetition
4. **Make it fun** - This is about enjoying fall together
5. **Adapt to your pace** - Stretch activities over multiple weeks if needed

---

## Customization Ideas

- Add family traditions and celebrations
- Incorporate your child's interests
- Use your local fall activities and events
- Connect to your cultural heritage
- Extend activities based on curiosity

---

## Assessment
This unit uses **observation and participation** rather than testing.

Look for:
- Engagement in activities
- Vocabulary use in conversations
- Confidence with basic skills
- Enjoyment of learning
